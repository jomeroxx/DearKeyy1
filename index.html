To Myself,

Some days feel heavier than they should. Some nights you wonder if you’re enough, if the quiet means you’ve stopped growing.

But look at you. You’re still here. Still choosing to try. Still opening your eyes even when the weight sits on your chest.

You don’t need to be perfect to be worthy. You don’t need all the answers to deserve rest. The soft, tired version of you that keeps showing up is already enough.

Be gentle with the parts of you that are still healing.

The path ahead doesn’t ask for perfection — only that you keep walking. Slowly is still progress. Quietly is still brave.

I’m proud of you. Even on the days you can’t feel it yet. Especially then.

— with all my love
